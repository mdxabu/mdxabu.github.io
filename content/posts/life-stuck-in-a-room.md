Sometimes, life feels like being stuck in a room not just a room with four walls, but a room inside our own minds. Every day feels the same, every moment feels heavy, and even the things that once made us happy seem to lose their magic.

We wake up, look at the same walls, scroll through our phones, and watch the world move forward while we feel left behind. There are dreams we want to chase, places we want to visit, and people we want to meet, but somehow, we remain in the same place.

A room can be peaceful, but sometimes it becomes a prison for our thoughts. The silence gets louder, loneliness becomes familiar, and overthinking becomes a daily habit.

But maybe this phase of life isn't the end of our story. Maybe it's just a quiet chapter, teaching us things we couldn't learn in a crowded world. Even when life feels stuck, time keeps moving, and so can we one small step at a time.

Because life isn't meant to be lived within four walls forever. Someday, we'll open that door, breathe fresh air, and realize that the world is still waiting for us.
