I don’t want to talk.<br>
I don’t want to explain anything.<br>
I don’t even know what to say.

I just need some quiet.

Every conversation feels tiring.<br>
Every notification feels like pressure.

It’s not that I hate people.

Sometimes, you just get tired<br>
of trying to be someone<br>
everyone can understand.

So let me be alone for a while.

No calls.<br>
No texts.<br>
No expectations.

Just me, my thoughts,<br>
and a little peace.

Maybe being alone isn’t loneliness.

Maybe it's where<br>
you finally hear yourself again.
